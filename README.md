IngredientOS

A Smart Grocery Management and Food Waste Reduction Platform

About the Project

IngredientOS is a full-stack grocery management platform designed to help people manage their groceries from the moment they purchase them until they are consumed.

With the growth of online grocery shopping and quick-commerce services, purchasing groceries has become faster and more convenient. However, an important problem remains: what happens after groceries are delivered?

People often purchase more groceries than they need, forget about ingredients already available at home, reorder products they already have, or fail to use perishable items before they lose freshness.

As a result, food that could have been consumed often goes to waste.

IngredientOS aims to address this problem by connecting grocery shopping with household inventory management. It helps users keep track of their purchases, monitor ingredient freshness, discover recipes using available ingredients, and make informed grocery decisions.

The goal is not just to make grocery shopping easier, but also to encourage responsible purchasing and reduce food waste after delivery.

Problem We Aim to Solve

Online grocery shopping provides convenience, but managing purchased groceries efficiently remains a challenge.

IngredientOS focuses on three important problems:

1. Unnecessary Purchasing:** Buying ingredients that are already available at home.
2. Forgotten Ingredients:** Losing track of groceries stored in refrigerators and kitchens.
3. Food Waste:** Allowing ingredients to lose freshness without being consumed.

By bringing grocery shopping, inventory tracking, freshness monitoring, and recipe recommendations together, IngredientOS aims to reduce these problems.

Project Objective

The primary objective of IngredientOS is to build a centralized grocery management system that helps users:

- Purchase groceries more responsibly.
- Track ingredients available at home.
- Monitor estimated freshness.
- Utilize existing ingredients effectively.
- Reduce unnecessary grocery purchases.
- Minimize avoidable food waste.
